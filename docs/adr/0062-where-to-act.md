# ADR 0062: Where to act

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-09-22 · **Decided by** the product owner ("a button …
that will give suggestions and actionable points to the board members: what can be done, what
can be improved, according to the business and on the basis of data") · **Neighbour of** SPEC §25
commentary

## Context

Commentary already reads the month. Its prompt forbids advice in as many words — *"No hype, no
advice framed as professional opinion"* — and that is deliberate: a board pack that mixes what
happened with what to do about it in one paragraph is the thing a client cannot act on, and the
thing a chartered accountant cannot put their name to.

So the ask is not a bigger commentary. It is the other half of the page, and it is a separate
document, a separate decision to buy, and a separate AI call.

## Decisions

### 1. It is called "Where to act", and it is its own priced action

One button in the assistant, beside "Write the commentary", against the same month selector. It
creates a `board_actions` job, holds its credits, runs the stage in the request and captures on
delivery — the same machine every other paid action runs on, so a platform fault releases the
whole hold and a quote over the AI cost cap stops it before it spends (locked decisions 3, 5, 6).

Priced like commentary, base 149 with the 49 instant surcharge **outside** the tier multiplier,
because it is the same size of job on the same facts pack. `TODO(review)` against R-04/R-05: the
number is illustrative until the live evals show what the call costs.

It borrows commentary's `commentary_queued` / `commentary_done` states rather than adding two of
its own. Those states mean "an AI write-up is queued" and "it is written", which is exactly what
happens here, and `completeCommentaryJob` has never looked at the job's type.

### 2. It reads the same facts pack commentary reads

`factsPackFor` is now shared. This is what makes locked decision 7 hold: every figure the model
might cite already exists as a fact with an id, so it writes `{{m:revenue@2026-04}}` and the
engine substitutes the figure **it** computed. The check is `checkPlaceholderTexts`, the same one
the commentary and the chat answer go through, run over every field the model wrote — the
summary, and each action's heading, reason and step.

A suggestion containing a number the model typed is a validation failure. Not a number to strip
out, not a warning: the stage fails and one repair is attempted, as every stage does.

Two stages reading two subtly different sets of figures about the same month is the failure this
avoids. There is one builder.

### 3. An action is a heading, a reason and a step

- `heading` — the thing itself, no figures.
- `because` — what in the books says so. This is where the placeholders go.
- `todo` — what to do. **Required**: the check refuses an empty one, because an observation with
  no step attached is commentary, and commentary already exists.
- `urgency` — `now`, `this_quarter` or `watch`. Three words rather than a score, because a
  numeric priority would be a figure the model made up.

Five by default (`board_actions.max_actions`, config), bounded 3–8 by the schema whatever the
config says.

### 4. What it will not do

The prompt refuses generic playbooks, forbids inventing a fact, and says plainly that these are
suggestions for the board to consider — **not tax, legal, audit or investment advice**. The panel
says the same thing under the list, where the reader is. For a product sold to CA firms that
sentence is not decoration.

## Not active yet

No prompt version is active for `board_actions` in `tier_routing`, exactly as none is for
`commentary`, `ledger_mapping` or any other stage. Until the owner runs its evals (R-28) the
button is there and the stage refuses, which fails the job as a platform fault and releases the
hold in full — nobody is charged for an answer they did not get. That is the same gate every AI
stage in this product sits behind, not a gap in this one.

## What this does not do

- **It does not run on its own.** No suggestion is generated by a refresh, because a monthly
  refresh on unchanged structure makes no AI call at all and that zero is the recurring margin
  (ADR 0052). Somebody presses the button.
- **It does not know anything the MIS does not.** No market data, no benchmarks, no industry
  packs — those are out of scope (SPEC §3). It reasons over this company's own books.
- **It is not stored on the dashboard.** It reads in the assistant beside the commentary and in
  History, like the commentary does.

## Tests

`packages/ai/test/board-actions.test.ts`: a suggestion whose figures are placeholders over facts
that exist is accepted; a figure the model wrote itself is refused, as a numeral, as a word, and
in the action as well as the reason; a placeholder for a metric this company does not hold is
refused; the summary and the heading are checked, not only the body; an action with nothing to do
is refused, and so are more actions than were asked for.

`packages/ai/test/surface.test.ts` refused the new export until it was registered as a
purpose-named function — the rule in `.claude/rules/ai-boundary.md` working as designed.
