# SEO keyword research: every word a buyer types, by market

Dated 2026-10-05. Companion to [seo-marketing.md](seo-marketing.md), which records the pages
already built and the rules they follow. This document does not change any page; it says which
words exist, who already owns them, and where a new page or a new heading would earn its place.

## Method, and what it cannot tell you

**What was measured.**

- **Google autocomplete**, harvested from `suggestqueries.google.com` for about 450 seed phrases,
  with the `gl` country parameter set in turn to US, GB, AU, NZ, CA, IE, SG, AE, ZA and IN. A
  phrase Google completes is one enough people type for Google to predict; a seed that completes
  to something else (`ai mis` → "ai mission", `automated mis` → "automated misting system") is
  one almost nobody types.
- **Bing autocomplete** (`api.bing.com/osjson.aspx`) for 36 core seeds in nine markets, as a
  second, independent engine.
- **Live search results** for about 50 queries: who ranks, what the competitors call
  themselves, and which competitors pay for "alternative" landing pages (Fathom runs paid pages
  for "Reach Reporting alternative" and "Syft Analytics alternative" — direct evidence that these
  queries convert).
- **Competitor title tags, H1s and meta descriptions**, read from 40 home pages.

**What it cannot tell you.**

- **There are no search volumes in this document, and none have been invented.** There is no paid
  keyword tool. Every demand rating is qualitative and says what it rests on.
- **Autocomplete was harvested from an Indian IP address.** The country parameter changed only a
  handful of completions (Bing's market parameter changed nothing at all), so regional
  differences below come mainly from **who ranks and what local firms call the work**, not from
  autocomplete. Indian bias shows in the data — "accounts management software company in
  bhubaneswar" appears under a US seed. Re-run the harvest from a UK or US address before
  trusting a regional difference that rests on autocomplete alone.
- **Google Trends refused every request (HTTP 429)** and **Reddit blocks unauthenticated
  search**, so neither trend lines nor forum phrasing were read directly. Forum language below
  comes from AccountingWEB, UK Business Forums and the ICAI AI use-case pages, which were
  reachable.
- Search results were fetched from a US-only search endpoint; UK, Australian and Indian result
  pages may differ.

**Rating scales.**

| Rating      | Meaning                                                                                                           |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| High        | The seed completes to itself plus many on-topic long-tail variants, and the results are crowded with big players. |
| Medium      | The seed completes to itself with several on-topic variants.                                                      |
| Low         | The seed completes only to itself, or to one or two variants.                                                     |
| Emerging    | An "AI + X" phrase with several variants already ("…generator", "…free", "best ai for…") and young results.       |
| None        | No completion, or the phrase is captured by another meaning. A brand line, not a search term.                     |
| Competition | Who ranks: **Giant** (Microsoft, Intuit, Xero, Sage, Oracle), **SaaS** (funded vertical tools), **Directory** (G2, Capterra, GetApp, alternativeTo), **Blogs** (firms, templates, listicles), **Video** (YouTube). |

Intent: **C** = commercial (wants a tool), **I** = informational (wants to learn), **N** =
navigational (wants a named product), **T** = template (wants a file to download).

## The findings that matter most

1. **The phrases the site was built around have almost no search demand.** "AI MIS", "MIS with
   AI", "MIS in minutes" and "automated MIS" return nothing on-topic in either engine. They are
   good lines for the product to say, and they are not what anyone types. What people **do** type
   nearby is "MIS automation", "MIS report automation", "MIS report using AI", "AI tool for MIS
   report" and "MIS report generator" — all Low or Emerging, all India.
2. **"AI management accounts" is read by Google as "AI account management".** Its results are
   about sales account managers. The page `/ai-management-accounts` is sound, but its title must
   carry "monthly management accounts" for Google to place it at all, and its traffic will be
   small whatever is done.
3. **The biggest gap in the West is the accounting system's name.** "Management accounts in Xero",
   "Xero management report pack", "QuickBooks management reports", "how to produce management
   accounts on Sage 50" and "MYOB management reports" all autocomplete, and the site has no page
   for any of them — the plan deferred them until menu paths were verified (SPEC §0.4). That is
   the single most valuable piece of work this research points to.
4. **Competitor "alternative" pages are a real, contested, winnable cluster.** "Fathom reporting
   alternatives", "Syft Analytics alternatives", "Reach Reporting alternatives", "Fathom vs Spotlight
   Reporting" and "Syft vs Fathom" all autocomplete, and Fathom pays for its own alternative
   landing pages. Every competitor named here sells a monthly subscription, most priced by the number of companies; prepaid
   credits that never expire is a difference a comparison page can state plainly.
5. **The "AI + Excel + finance" family is where a new site can win early.** "AI dashboard from
   Excel", "excel to dashboard ai", "AI financial report generator", "AI balance sheet generator",
   "AI profit and loss generator", "AI trial balance generator", "can AI prepare financial
   statements", "can ChatGPT create financial statements" and "is it safe to upload financial
   statements to ChatGPT" all autocomplete with many variants, and the results are young tools
   and consumer articles, not giants.
6. **"Trial balance to financial statements" is a large, stable, every-market phrase** ("…in
   excel", "…converter", "mapping trial balance to financial statements in excel", "automation of
   financial statements from trial balance"). The site answers it only under the narrower
   "trial balance to management report".
7. **India's demand is enormous but polluted.** "MIS report" completes first to government
   schemes (NREGA, PMAY-G, panchayat); the business demand is in the long tail — "MIS report
   format in excel for accounts and finance", "…for manufacturing company", "monthly MIS report
   format in excel for accounts", "MIS report in Tally Prime", and the Hinglish "MIS report kaise
   banaye", "excel me MIS report kaise banaye", "Tally me MIS report kaise banaye", which are
   genuinely typed. "Tally AI" is a new, growing family with no dominant owner.
8. **The UAE searches in India's words.** "MIS report meaning in accounting", "MIS report in
   Tally" and "MIS reporting jobs in Dubai" complete under the UAE setting; Indian-run businesses
   there use Tally. The India pages serve the UAE as they stand.
