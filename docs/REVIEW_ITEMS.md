The wording and §34's time limit stay with R-10/R-12. | Before launch | **closed** |
| R-79 | **A refund removes the sale from the accounting exports** (ADR 0057). `accountingCsv("credits_sold")` filters `status = 'credited'`, so a purchase refunded later vanishes from the month it was sold in, while its tax invoice stays in the GST summary and the credits stay in the customer's wallet. Three reports that should reconcile do not, and `business.ts` loses cash that was actually received. Record the refund beside the sale rather than in place of it. **Closed 2026-09-21 by ADR 0058:** the sale keeps its status and the refund is recorded beside it, so it stays in the month it was sold in; `credits_sold` carries a `refunded` column and `gst_summary` reports credit notes as their own rows. | Before the first refund | **closed** |
| R-80 | **A partial refund marks the whole purchase refunded** (ADR 0057). `razorpayWebhookSchema` does not parse `payload.refund.entity`, so any `refund.*` event flags the purchase whatever the amount. Read the refunded amount and distinguish partial from full. **Closed 2026-09-21 by ADR 0058:** the refund's own entity is parsed, partial refunds accumulate, and refunds are de-duplicated by the refund id rather than the event id — one refund reaches us as three events. | Before the first refund | **closed** |
| R-81 | **The LUT ARN is not tied to a financial year** (ADR 0057). Form GST RFD-11 is filed once per financial year, and `billing.export.lut_arn` keeps returning last year's ARN into the new one. An export invoice carrying an ARN from a year it does not cover is not under a valid LUT, which makes IGST payable on that supply. Key the ARN by financial year, or refuse to issue when the configured one was not recorded for the invoice's year. Depends on R-59. **Closed 2026-09-21 by ADR 0058:** `billing.export.lut_by_fy` maps a financial year to the ARN filed for it, and a sale into a year with no ARN recorded is refused before the money moves. Still needs R-59 for the real numbers. | Before the first April after launch | **closed** |
| R-82 | **The auth throttle checks and increments separately** (ADR 0057). `checkThrottle` reads outside any transaction and `registerFailure` locks only afterwards, so a burst of parallel attempts all read zero and all pass: 200 concurrent sign-ins get 200 guesses against a limit of 10 before the key locks. With no customer second factor this throttle is what stands between a leaked password list and an account. Make check and increment one statement. **Closed 2026-09-21 by ADR 0058:** `claimAttempt` counts and decides in one locked decision, and `releaseAttempt` gives a successful attempt back to the network's key so an office cannot lock itself out. | Before launch | **closed** |
| R-83 | **The chat can add a box for a split the books do not make** (ADR 0057). `chat_edit` is checked against the metric catalog, not against what this company holds, so "add payroll by department" for a company with no department split is charged and renders "Nothing to split this month" for ever. The `dashboard_layout` stage is held to the stricter rule because nobody asked for its boxes. Giving `chat_edit` the company's splits is a prompt change and therefore an eval. Depends on R-28. **Closed 2026-09-21 by ADR 0058:** `chatEditInput` carries the splits the company's books make and the check refuses a box that uses any other, judged only on what the change brings in. Prompt v3 tells the model, and waits on R-28 like every other version. | With the next chat prompt version | **closed** |
